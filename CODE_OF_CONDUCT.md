# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Afterimage welcomes contributors of all backgrounds. Keep discussions constructive, respectful, curious, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas.
- Welcome beginners and explain unfamiliar concepts.
- Critique code and proposals, not people.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information
- Deliberate disruption or malicious behavior
- Sexualized or hateful conduct
- Sharing private user memory or secrets

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
